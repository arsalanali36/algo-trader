Sab Intraday Positional ✅ Sirf pass wali

🔗 Correlation — kaun kiske saath chalti hai (daily net P&L, ③ BS pass)

+1 = bilkul saath chalti hain (diversification ZERO) · 0 = koi rishta nahi (best mix) · −ve = ek girti hai to doosri badhti hai (hedge). Portfolio ke liye 0 ke paas ya −ve jodi chahiye.

📖 Terms — aasaan bhasha me

Sharpe — return ÷ risk. Jitna zyada utna smooth paisa. Gate: ≥ 1. (2+ = bahut achha)
p-value — "ye edge LUCK to nahi?" 1000 random timings se compare. p < 0.05 = luck nahi, asli edge. p = 0.000 matlab ek bhi random timing isse behtar nahi tha.
Train / OOS — data ke 2 hisse: TRAIN (purana ~68%, jispe params optimize hue) aur OOS = Out-Of-Sample (recent ~32%, jo optimizer ne KABHI nahi dekha). OOS hi asli imtihaan hai — recent regime ka primary judge.
Robust — train aur OOS Sharpe me se JO KAM ho. Ranking isi pe hoti hai taaki strategy dono jagah chale, sirf ek me nahi (overfit-guard).
Max DD (drawdown) — equity peak se sabse bada gaddha. Gate: ≤ 20%. Chhota DD = raat ko neend achhi.
Win% + PF — Win% = kitne trades jeete. PF (profit factor) = total jeet ÷ total haar (>1 = profitable). Kam Win% + high PF = "kam baar jeeto, badi jeeto" (hamari chain strategies aisi hi hain).
Exit styles — stop_only: sirf stop-loss, koi target nahi, winner ko chalne do. atr_rr: ATR stop + fixed target. trail: peeche-peeche chalta stop. flip: ulta signal aane pe exit.
hold (max_hold_days) — positional me position max kitne DIN rakhni hai (e.g. hold 3 = teesre din tak zabardasti exit).
touch_tol / zone_age / max_cs / hawa / lookback — chain-zone knobs: line-touch tolerance (pts) / key-candle ke baad kitne bars tak breakout valid / breakout candle max size (pts) / hawa-me zone on-off / chain kitne din peeche.
③ BS pass — asli deployable P&L: option premium (Black-Scholes) + real Zerodha charges. Spot wala ① sirf "edge hai ya nahi" batata hai.
MC median — 1000 baar trades ko shuffle karke dekha; original result median ke PAAS hona chahiye (best-5% me ho to overfit ka shak).
