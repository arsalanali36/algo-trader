⚠️ Max Loss Protection

            Global → Per-Strategy → Per-Trade
          

          

            3 level pe kaam karta hai: Global default (yahan) →
            Per-Strategy override (neeche "Per-Strategy Override" accordion, blank =
            global) →
            Per-Trade manual (kisi bhi open position ke ⚙️ modal se, hamesha sabse upar
            priority).
          

          

            
              

                1️⃣ Strategy Day Cap
                  Pura din ke liye — cumulative P&L itna ₹/% loss cross kare to poori
                    strategy
                    ke naye entries ruk jaate hain + open positions squareoff. PAPER: ₹ cap use hota hai. LIVE: % cap
                    broker ke REAL Total Margin ka % hota hai (fresh balance se, kabhi stale nahi).
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

              

                🎯 Daily Profit Target
                  Aaj ka combined realized P&L is target ko cross kare → saari open
                    positions
                    immediately squareoff + us din koi nayi entry nahi. Blank = off.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

              

                2️⃣ Per-Trade Default SL & Target
                  Har naye individual position ke khulte hi ye default Stop-Loss aur Target
                    (TP) automatically lag jaata hai — sirf wahi ek leg squareoff hoti hai, baaki strategy/instrument pe
                    asar nahi.
                
                
                  

                    

                      

                        
                        
                      

                      

                        
                        
                      

                    

                    

                      
                      
                    

                    

                      
⚙️ Premium-based Trailing Steps (Custom /
                        Default):

                      

                        

                          
                          
                        

                        

                          
                          
                        

                        

                          
                          
                        

                        

                          
                          
                        

                      

                      * Leave blank to use defaults. If
                        you enter a custom value, it overrides the default step size for that premium band.
                    


                    

                      

                        
                        
                      

                      

                        
                        
                      

                    

                    

                      
                      
                    

                  

                		
              

              

                3️⃣ Per-Instrument Trailing Lock
                    🔒
                  Har open position ka apna alag arm+gap+confirm floor — ek position ka
                    profit
                    uske peak se Gap ₹ neeche aaye (Arm ₹ cross karne ke baad) aur Confirm sec lagatar wahi rahe → sirf
                    WAHI position squareoff, baaki sab chalte rehte hain. Same design jaisa KILL-ALL (4️⃣), sirf scope
                    account ki jagah ek position tak.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

              

                4️⃣ 🔒 KILL-ALL Profit Floor
                  Account-level trailing kill-floor. MTM Arm ₹ cross kare → floor arm; floor
                    =
                    confirmed peak − Gap ₹, kabhi neeche nahi aata. MTM floor ke neeche Confirm sec lagatar rahe →
                    SAARI positions close + poore din entries block. Spike/whipsaw pe kabhi fire nahi hota.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

            
          

        


        
        

          
💰 Capital Allocation

          

            Global capital — sab strategies milake itne se zyada deploy nahi hoga. Jo signal capital na hone ki wajah se
            nahi li gayi, woh Orders & P&L me "🚫 Capital" tag ke saath dikhegi (real order nahi jaata).
            Margin Multiplier: option-SELL karne me premium se zyada margin block hota hai (estimate) — SELL legs ka
            "capital used" notional se zyada dikhega (e.g. 5 = qty×premium×5). BUY legs affect nahi hote. Default 1
            (off).
          

          

            
              

                Capital Ceilings & Sizing
                  Total Capital ₹ = always-on per-position rule: 1% loss cross kare to
                    force-squareoff, source/SL tags se independent.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

            
          

        


        
        

          
🧪 Shadow-Live Testing (DANGEROUS — OFF by default)

          

            ON karne par har PAPER trade ke saath ek REAL Dhan order bhi parallel me fire hoga (same price/qty) — sirf
            fill/reject compare karne ke liye (slippage check). Paper P&L is se kabhi affect nahi hota. Sirf tab ON
            karo jab Dhan account balance zero/near-zero ho (warna real order fill ho sakta hai!). Test khatam hote hi
            OFF kar dena.
          

          

            
              

                Shadow-Live Mode
                  Dangerous parallel execution — real order fires alongside paper.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

            
          

        


        
        

          
🛡️ Auto-Hedge (naked option SELL → auto BUY further OTM)

          

            Jab koi strategy option SELL karti hai, uske turant baad ek hedge BUY same option type, further OTM,
            automatically jaata hai. Min Strikes = kam se kam itne strikes ATM/sold-leg se door. Max Premium ₹
            (optional) = hedge Min Strikes se aur door chalta rehta hai jab tak premium is ₹ se kam/equal na mil jaye —
            jo bhi door wala strike mile, wahi use hota hai. Dono khali = hedge OFF.
          

          

            
              

                Option Hedging
                  Strike offsets and premium filters.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

            
          

        


        
        

          
📊 Live Liquidity Filter (any 2-of-3, ON by default)

          

            Static stock-whitelist se zyada flexible — har entry se pehle exact option contract ka live market depth
            check: Spread% ≤ 1.5, Volume lots ≥ 500 (≥50 before 9:45 AM), OI lots ≥ 300. Teeno mein se kam se kam 2 pass
            karna kaafi hai. Live data na mile to FAIL-OPEN hota hai (block nahi karta) — illiquid-contract guard hai,
            P&L-correctness guard nahi.
          

          

            
              

                Contract Liquidity Guard
                  Live bid-ask spread & volume limits.
                
                
                  

                    

                      
                    

                  

                		
              

            
          

        


        
        

          
💰 Broker Balances & Position Book

          

            Live cash + collateral, dono brokers se. Total Margin (cash+collateral) isi se LIVE strategies ka
            "Global Max Loss %" cap calculate hota hai (⚠️ Max Loss Protection tab dekho).

            chal raha ho to unrealized P&L "—" dikhega.

📖 Reasons For Exit

          

            Completed Trades table ke "Exit Reason" column me ye sab values aa sakti hain — yahan sab
            ek jagah, kis wajah se aata hai uske saath. _GROUP suffix wala matlab: ye leg
            apne hedge-pair sibling ke saath auto-close hui, khud apne trigger se nahi.
          

          


          
🛡️ Har open position pe lagu —
            koi bhi strategy ho (pos_monitor_loop)

          

            
              

                🛑 Stop-Loss
                Per-trade SL hit (⚠️ Max Loss Protection
                  tab → 2️⃣ Per-Trade Default SL, ya position ke apne ⚙️ modal se override)
              

              

                🎯 Target
                Per-trade Target hit (same modal, TP
                  side)
              

              

                ⏰ 3:15 EOD
                Blanket 3:15 PM squareoff — har open
                  OPTION position, koi exception nahi
              

              

                📅 Expiry EOD (2:55)
                Aaj expiry day hai — normal 3:15 se 20
                  min pehle hi squareoff (last-hour chaos avoid)
              

              

                📅 Expiry ITM
                Expiry day pe sold option ITM ho gaya —
                  turant exit, 2:55 ka bhi wait nahi
              

              

                ⚠️ RMS Max-Loss
                Din ka combined loss cap cross ho gaya
                  (Max Loss Protection → 1️⃣ Strategy Day Cap) — poori strategy squareoff
              

              

                ✅ RMS Profit Target
                Din ka combined profit target hit (🎯
                  Daily Profit Target) — poori strategy squareoff
              

              

                🔒 Kill-Floor
                Account-level KILL-ALL profit floor
                  fired (4️⃣ card) — saari positions, poore din ke liye entries block
              

              

                🔒 Trailing Lock
                Per-Instrument Trailing Lock (3️⃣ card)
                  fire hua — sirf yehi position squareoff, baaki entries block nahi hoti
              

              

                🚨 No-Price Emergency
                Position ka price 60+ cycles (~5 min) se
                  kahin se nahi mil raha (feed+REST+cache sab fail) — emergency exit koshish
