6 max-3 vs max-5 — ek achhi behes

User ne kaha max-3 lo (kam trade + OOS zyada). Maine max-5 argue kiya. Dono side ke asli point:

max-3 ke liye: higher OOS (+15,449 vs 12,547), 65 kam trades (kam cost), "trade less" thesis ke saath, uncertain 4th-5th trade se door.
max-5 ke liye: net post-cost zyada (extra trades ne apni cost nikaali + profit diya); bade 11-din train pe solid; max-1 sabse kam (+9,750) = "kam hamesha behtar" GALAT, ek floor hai.
Faisla (user): paper phase me goal = max data collect → max-5 zyada observations deta, aur 5 se aage edge nahi. Data badhne pe khud 3-vs-5 settle ho jaayega, "bina emotion, pure logic pe." Chuna: max 5.
