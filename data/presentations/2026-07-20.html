"Mujhe market structure se pehle dikh jaata hai kya ho raha, entry sahi hoti — par phir wo entry profit de kar fail ho jaati. Toh main khud cut karna chahta hoon, kahin bhi — app se, Kite se. Bas koi tamasha na ho."
