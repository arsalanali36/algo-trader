TASK 0 · STATISTICAL GUARDRAILS
d2b8da5

Pehle "jhooth pakadne wali" machine banai.

Mining se ek line pehle — 5 rails, taaki scale pe luck edge ka bhes na badal sake.

rail Deflated Sharpe (DSR)
Bar ab isse tay hoti hai ki kitne candidate try kiye — jitne zyada attempts, utna hi ooncha proof chahiye. Winner-only nahi, poori search count hoti hai.
rail Purged walk-forward CV
Train/test mein 1-din purge + 5-din embargo — ek hi market-move overlapping windows se future ka info "OOS" mein leak na ho.
rail Hard OOS lockbox
Last ~60 din (≥ 15 Apr) ka data tijori mein band — koi tuning use nahi kar sakti. Sirf gate-clear candidate ek baar test karta hai. Fail = maut, re-tune nahi.
rail Trials log
Har candidate — winners hi nahi, sab — ml_mining_log.csv mein. DSR ka number honest count pe bane, cherry-picked pe nahi.
🧪
Calibration proof: 1000 mein se best pure random noise (Sharpe 1.71 — purana gate isko pyaar karta) → DSR 0.34, reject. Shipped strategies → DSR 0.87–0.90. Gate saaf-saaf farak karta hai — noise andar nahi aata.
